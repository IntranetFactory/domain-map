# VDR Phase 0 — Vendor Surface

**Market:** Secure virtual data rooms for M&A due diligence, fundraising, and other transactions. A permissioned document repository plus a structured Q&A workflow, activity/audit tracking, redaction, and watermarking. Backs the Corporate Legal agents (Tabular Diligence Review, Data Room Watcher).

## Modularization hypothesis

VDR is not one flat module. Four masters + heavy lifecycle substrate push it to a **four-module domain** (Rule #14: >=3 capabilities => >=2 modules; here 4 natural clusters each with >=1 master):

1. **repository** — `data_rooms`, `room_folders`, `room_documents`, `document_versions`, `document_indexes`, plus room/document templates and `deal_stages`. The document-management substrate everything else hangs off.
2. **access_control** — `room_users`, `user_groups`, `permission_groups`, `permission_levels`, and the assignment/membership junctions, invitations, NDA gating, and notification subscriptions. The permission engine; heavyweight enough (levels + groups + per-object assignment matrix) to stand alone.
3. **qa_diligence** — `qa_threads`/`qa_questions`/`qa_answers` plus categories, role/expert assignments, status transitions, approval steps, and the diligence checklist family. A genuine workflow with states, approvals, and handoffs — the clearest split candidate.
4. **audit_analytics** — access logs, activity events, permission-change logs, engagement/heatmap analytics, bidder scores, and all compliance/retention entities. Legal-defensibility plus deal intelligence.

Protection primitives (`redaction_records`, `watermark_policies`, `document_expiry_rules`, `drm_restrictions`) are attributes of documents and access, not a standalone master-bearing cluster; I fold them into **repository**/**access_control** rather than mint a thin `protection` module (marked "protection" above only to flag their origin). If Phase A finds redaction accretes its own lifecycle (draft/applied/burned versions, reviewer sign-off), promote it then.

**Point-solution verdict:** VDR is a **genuine, well-bounded market** with a stable pure-play surface (Datasite, Intralinks, Ansarada, iDeals, Firmex all model the same core nouns). It is not a thin feature of DMS or CLM — the Q&A-diligence workflow and deal-audit trail are VDR-specific and absent from general document management. Load as a real domain.

**Own domain vs. part of M&A:** My read is **VDR as its own focused domain now**, not yet a module-family under a broader "M&A / transaction management" domain. Rationale: (a) the surface is self-contained around the room, not the deal — no deal pipeline, valuation, LOI/SPA drafting, or post-merger integration entities appear; (b) the two backing agents (Tabular Diligence Review, Data Room Watcher) act on room contents, not deal lifecycle; (c) a "transaction management" domain (deal pipeline, target lists, synergy tracking, closing checklists at the corporate level) is a distinct, larger market (e.g. DealCloud, Midaxo) that would *consume* VDR as a sibling, not absorb it. Recommendation: model VDR standalone; if a transaction-management domain is loaded later, relate the two via `solution_domains`/cross-domain links rather than nesting VDR inside it. `deal_stages`/`deal_audit_trails` are the only entities that hint at the wider deal context, and they belong to the room's own view of the deal, not a separate pipeline.
