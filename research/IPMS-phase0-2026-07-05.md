## Modularization hypothesis

Six modules, each with >=1 master; a >=3-capability domain requires >=2 modules (Rule #14 satisfied, well exceeded). No vendor names appear in any entity name (Rule #18). Names avoid collision with the live catalog's adjacent legal domain (`legal_matters`, `in_house_legal_matters`, `legal_case_dockets`) by using IP-specific prefixes (`ip_matters`, `docket_entries`).

- **patent_management** — masters patent-side assets and prosecution nouns: `patent_families`, `patent_applications`, `granted_patents`, `invention_disclosures`, `inventors`, `priority_claims`, `fto_analyses`.
- **trademark_management** — masters brand-side assets and clearance: `trademark_applications`, `trademark_registrations`, `goods_and_services_classes`, `clearance_searches`, `conflict_watch_notices`.
- **docketing_and_deadlines** — the statutory-deadline engine and workflow substrate: `docket_entries`, `statutory_deadlines`, `office_actions`, `prosecution_events`, `status_transitions`, `ip_office_status_syncs`, `docketing_rule_sets`, `docket_audit_trails`. First-class compliance module.
- **renewals_and_annuities** — maintenance-fee/annuity lifecycle: `annuity_records`, `renewal_instructions`, `maintenance_fee_payments`, `lapse_decisions`, `jurisdiction_fee_schedules`, `agent_network_assignments`.
- **ip_spend** — cost/budget tracking: `ip_spend_records`, `ip_invoices`, `cost_estimates`, `ip_budgets`, `foreign_agents`.
- **portfolio_management** — cross-asset master hub and shared substrate: `ip_assets`, `ip_matters`, `ip_owners`, `jurisdictions`, `design_rights`, `domain_name_assets`, `trade_secret_records`, `chain_of_title_records`, `ip_documents`, `ip_correspondence`.

`docketing_and_deadlines` and `renewals_and_annuities` could arguably merge (both are deadline-driven), but they split cleanly: docketing tracks prosecution/statutory response deadlines, renewals track a distinct recurring-payment lifecycle with its own agent-network and fee-schedule substrate. Keep separate pending Phase A.
