## Diff vs live footprint

### MISSING
Entities in the vendor surface that are not among the 7 current ITSM masters. Six are genuine net-new master candidates; the rest are out-of-domain or sub-master.

1. **service_releases**: release & deployment record distinct from a single change. Modeled by name in V4 Freshservice (`/releases` object), V5 ManageEngine (Release & Deployment module), V3 BMC (release coordination), V1 ServiceNow (Release Management), V6 Ivanti. Proposed module: ITSM-CHANGE-MGMT (or a new ITSM-RELEASE module). Classification: Core.
2. **service_offerings**: the back-end service definition under a business service, separate from its user-facing catalog item. Modeled by name in V1 ServiceNow CSDM ("two completely separate objects," catalog_item to service_offering is m:m), V3 BMC, V4 Freshservice (services vs catalog presentation). Proposed module: ITSM-SERVICE-REQUEST. Classification: Core.
3. **service_outages**: customer-facing outage / status record distinct from the internal incident. Modeled by name in V1 ServiceNow (Outage records childed to a major incident, shown on portal), V2 Atlassian Statuspage (Incidents + Components + Subscribers public page), V4 Freshservice (Freshstatus public outage history: Operational / Degraded / Partial Outage / Major Outage). No collision with `outage_events` (id 665, UTIL-OPS grid outage). Proposed module: ITSM-INCIDENT-MGMT. Classification: Core.
4. **cab_meetings**: Change Advisory Board agenda + minutes as a first-class record. Modeled by name in V1 ServiceNow (CAB Workbench: scheduled meetings, digital agenda, conflict calendar, risk scores), V3 BMC, V4 Freshservice (CAB scheduling). Proposed module: ITSM-CHANGE-MGMT. Classification: Core.
5. **change_collisions**: detection of overlapping change windows / blackout conflicts. Modeled by name in V1 ServiceNow (Change Management Collision Detector plugin: simple/advanced conflict detection against blackout and maintenance schedules), V3 BMC (change scheduling/conflict review). Proposed module: ITSM-CHANGE-MGMT. Classification: Common.
6. **virtual_agent_conversations**: conversational/virtual-agent transcripts. Modeled by name in V1 ServiceNow (Interaction table + Transcript field via Virtual Agent), V4 Freshservice (Freddy AI Agent conversations that spawn tickets), V5 ManageEngine (AI virtual agent), V2 Jira SM (virtual agent / Atlassian Intelligence). Proposed module: ITSM-AGENT-WORKSPACE. Classification: Core.
7. **walkup_visits**: walk-up / onsite support check-in record (queue position, kiosk, estimated service time). Modeled by name in V1 ServiceNow (Walk-Up Experience: check into a walk-up queue, see position + estimated service time + outage info). Not modeled as a first-class entity by V2-V5. Proposed module: ITSM-AGENT-WORKSPACE. Classification: Specialist.

Out-of-domain MISSING (vendors model these inside the ITSM product but they are owned by other Semantius domains, listed so they are not mistaken for ITSM gaps): configuration_items / service_maps (CMDB / DISCOVERY), service_assets and service_contracts and purchase_orders (ITAM / procurement), chargeback_records (ITFM / SPM). These should NOT be loaded as ITSM masters.

### WRONG-OWNERSHIP
- **chargeback_records / cost_charges**: every flagship that ships it (V1 ServiceNow Financial Charging / Showback) places it in IT Financial Management or Service Portfolio Management, billing a *business service*, not the ITSM ticket workflow. The ITSM-CHARGEBACK capability is a realize-only thin link; the master, if loaded anywhere, belongs to an ITFM/SPM domain, not ITSM. Recommendation: leave ITSM-CHARGEBACK as a capability-orphan or relabel it as a cross-domain consumer; do not mint an ITSM-owned master.
- **service_events / alerts**: all five vendors expose an Event Management module, but in market practice the alert ingestion + correlation substrate is the boundary between ITSM and ITOM/observability (V1 ServiceNow positions Event Management in ITOM, not core ITSM). The event-to-incident trigger is ITSM; the raw alert master leans ITOM. See MODULARIZATION.
- No live ITSM master is positively owned by another domain in practice. The 7 current masters (incidents, requests, catalog items, problems, changes, knowledge, SLAs) all sit where flagship vendors put them.

### SCOPE-CREEP
- None among the 7 current masters. Each is supported by all five vendors as an ITSM-owned entity. There is no entity ITSM currently carries that the vendor surface refuses to recognize as ITSM. (The risk is the inverse: capability-orphans ITSM-CHARGEBACK / ITSM-VIRTUAL-AGENT / ITSM-WALKUP advertise scope with no backing master, which is a coverage gap, not creep.)

### MODULARIZATION
- **ITSM-EVENT-MGMT (module 42)** is the weakest fit. Event/alert correlation is the ITSM/ITOM seam; flagship suites (ServiceNow) house Event Management in ITOM. Keep the module (it has a master, satisfying Rule #14), but scope it narrowly to the *event-triggered-incident* bridge and treat the raw alert/monitoring stream as an inbound handoff from an ITOM/OBS domain rather than an ITSM-mastered stream.
- **RELEASE** does not need its own module. ServiceNow, Freshservice, ManageEngine, and Ivanti all bundle Release inside Change & Release Management. `service_releases` should land as a second master inside ITSM-CHANGE-MGMT, alongside `service_changes` + the new `cab_meetings` and `change_collisions`. A standalone ITSM-RELEASE module would be over-fragmentation and is not required by Rule #14.
- **CAB + collisions** thicken ITSM-CHANGE-MGMT correctly; that module currently has one master (service_changes) and gains cab_meetings (master) + change_collisions (junction). Healthy.
- **MAJOR-INCIDENT** is a flag/child relationship on service_incidents in every vendor (ServiceNow childs incidents to a Major Incident record; others use a major-incident boolean + workflow). It is a modularization non-issue: no new module, no separate master. Keep the ITSM-MAJOR-INCIDENT capability backed by service_incidents + a `is_major` flag and a self-referential parent.
- **WALKUP / VIRTUAL-AGENT** belong in ITSM-AGENT-WORKSPACE (module 44), which today has no dedicated master. Adding walkup_visits and virtual_agent_conversations gives that module its own masters and retires two of the three capability-orphans.

## Verdicts on the 7 primary candidates

1. **service_outages: CONFIRM (load it).** Distinct from the internal incident and from `outage_events` (id 665, UTIL-OPS grid). Modeled by name in ServiceNow (Outage records on the portal, childed to major incidents), Atlassian Statuspage (public Incidents + Components + Subscribers), and Freshservice/Freshstatus (public outage history). Module: ITSM-INCIDENT-MGMT. Core.
2. **change_collisions: CONFIRM (load it).** Modeled by name in ServiceNow (Change Management Collision Detector, blackout + maintenance schedules, simple/advanced conflict modes) and BMC Helix (change conflict review). Load as a transition/junction under ITSM-CHANGE-MGMT. Common.
3. **cab_meetings: CONFIRM (load it).** Modeled by name in ServiceNow (CAB Workbench: scheduled meetings, agenda, conflict calendar, risk scores), BMC, and Freshservice. First-class master under ITSM-CHANGE-MGMT. Core.
4. **service_offerings: CONFIRM (load it).** ServiceNow CSDM explicitly treats service offering and catalog item as two separate objects (m:m); BMC and Freshservice distinguish service definition from catalog presentation. Load as a master under ITSM-SERVICE-REQUEST, related to service_catalog_items. Core.
5. **walkup_visits: RESHAPE (load, Specialist, single master, drop walkup_kiosks).** Only ServiceNow models the Walk-Up Experience as a first-class queue/check-in record (position, estimated service time, kiosk). No mid-market vendor models it. Load walkup_visits under ITSM-AGENT-WORKSPACE to back ITSM-WALKUP, but skip a separate walkup_kiosks master (kiosk is a location/config attribute, not its own master). Specialist.
6. **virtual_agent_conversations: CONFIRM (load it).** Modeled by name in ServiceNow (Interaction/Transcript), Freshservice (Freddy AI Agent conversations), ManageEngine (AI virtual agent), and Jira SM. Load under ITSM-AGENT-WORKSPACE to back ITSM-VIRTUAL-AGENT. Use this name over chatbot_sessions. Core.
7. **chargeback_invoices / service_cost_records: REFUTE (skip as an ITSM master).** ServiceNow ships chargeback/showback as Financial Charging in IT Financial Management / SPM, billing a business service, not the ITSM ticket. No other flagship models it inside the ITSM ticket workflow. Leave ITSM-CHARGEBACK as a cross-domain capability link; the cost master belongs to an ITFM/SPM domain. WRONG-OWNERSHIP.

Bonus adjudications: **service_releases: CONFIRM** (Freshservice, ManageEngine, BMC, ServiceNow, Ivanti all model a release record; load under ITSM-CHANGE-MGMT, no new module). **major_incidents: REFUTE as a separate master / RESHAPE as a flag** (every vendor models it as a flag + parent on service_incidents, not a distinct table).

## Modularization hypothesis

The existing 8-module split is sound and needs no new module: RELEASE folds into ITSM-CHANGE-MGMT (which also absorbs cab_meetings and change_collisions), and MAJOR-INCIDENT stays a flag on service_incidents. The real work is filling capability-orphans by giving ITSM-AGENT-WORKSPACE its own masters (walkup_visits, virtual_agent_conversations) and adding the customer-facing service_outages and service_offerings masters. The one genuine boundary question is ITSM-EVENT-MGMT: keep it, but narrow it to the event-to-incident bridge and treat raw alert/monitoring streams as an ITOM/OBS handoff rather than ITSM-mastered data.
