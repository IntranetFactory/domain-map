# CRM Phase 0 vendor-surface report (2026-06-08)

Scope: verify the 13 `b3` research candidates carried in `audits/CRM/q-CRM.md` /
`state.yaml` against (a) flagship CRM vendor practice and (b) live catalog ownership.
Produced as the forcing step for the market-shape `b2` decisions per SKILL Rule #22.
Zero DB writes. CRM is domain id 69 (5 full modules: CRM-ACCT-MGT 46, CRM-LEAD-MGT 47,
CRM-PIPELINE-MGT 48, CRM-ACTIVITY 49, CRM-AI-COPILOT 50). 6 masters: customers 97,
crm_contacts 98, crm_leads 99, crm_opportunities 100, pipeline_stages 101,
sales_activities 102.

## Flagship vendors surveyed

Enterprise / mid-market: Salesforce Sales Cloud, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales,
Oracle Sales (Fusion), SAP Sales Cloud, HubSpot Sales Hub, Zoho CRM, Creatio Sales,
SugarCRM. SMB: Pipedrive, Freshsales, Close, Copper, Insightly. Adjacent specialists
that own the "belongs elsewhere" entities: Clari / Gong / BoostUp (revenue intelligence,
forecasting), Xactly / Varicent / CaptivateIQ / Salesforce Spiff (incentive comp /
commissions), Anaplan / Salesforce Maps (territory + quota planning), Salesforce PRM /
Microsoft Partner Center / Impartner / ZINFI (partner relationship management).

## Per-candidate verdicts

### GROUP A: genuinely CRM-native, additive, fits an existing module (discretionary b3)

1. **competitors** -> CRM-PIPELINE-MGT master, `entity_type=catalog` (named competitor
   companies), plus a relationship to crm_opportunities (competing-on). Core across every
   flagship.
2. **competitor_intelligence** -> CRM-PIPELINE-MGT master, `entity_type=operational_record`,
   `necessity=optional` (battlecards / win-loss intel; Pipedrive and several SMB tools omit it).
3. **lead_assignment_rules** -> CRM-LEAD-MGT master, `entity_type=catalog` (criteria -> owner
   config). Core across every flagship.
4. **account_hierarchies** -> CRM-ACCT-MGT. Two valid shapes: a self-referential
   `parent_customer_id` on customers (lightweight) or a first-class hierarchy entity / junction
   (market practice for matrixed parent-subsidiary trees). Recommend the junction so a customer
   can sit in more than one rollup.
5. **sales_playbooks** -> CRM-AI-COPILOT master, `entity_type=catalog` (structured rep guidance;
   the copilot surfaces next-best-action from them). Common; Salesforce / HubSpot / Dynamics all
   ship it.
6. **sales_territories** -> CRM-ACCT-MGT master (the operational territory definition + account
   assignment, as in Salesforce Enterprise Territory Management). The *quota allocation to
   territories* and territory-based comp belong to SALES-PERF; the operational membership is CRM.

### GROUP B: belongs to another domain, CRM consumes at most (NOT a CRM master)

7. **sales_forecasts** -> **resolved**: REV-INTEL already masters the forecast cluster. The only
   CRM-side action is an optional `consumer` / `derived` DMDO on `revenue_forecasts` (1010) for
   CRM-PIPELINE-MGT if forecast visibility inside the pipeline view is wanted (today CRM has no
   DMDO on it). No CRM master.
8. **sales_quotas** -> **SALES-PERF** (Sales Performance Management). SALES-PERF is an unbuilt
   stub; quotas land there when it is built. CRM is a consumer at most.
9. **commission_records** -> **SALES-PERF / ICM**. Specialist category (Xactly, Varicent,
   CaptivateIQ, Spiff). Not CRM. `commission_splits` (354) is RE-BROKERAGE-specific and does not
   serve the generic case.
10. **partner_relationships** -> **PRM**. PRM is an unbuilt stub. CRM may carry co-sell
    opportunity links as consumer rows, but partner records are PRM-mastered.

### GROUP C: module splits (these are b2 decisions, not b3 per Rule #21)

- **CRM-FORECAST module: REJECT.** The forecasting *records* are mastered by REV-INTEL, so a
  sixth CRM module would have no records of its own to master. CRM's existing CRM-FORECAST
  capability (249) is the in-pipeline projection view, correctly realized inside CRM-PIPELINE-MGT.
  No flagship ships a CRM forecasting module that masters forecast records distinct from the
  revenue-intelligence layer.
- **CRM-MARKETING-LIST module: REJECT.** `audience_segments` is mastered by CDP (72) and
  CRM-ACCT-MGT already consumes it. HubSpot / Zoho / Freshsales lists are a feature of contact
  management, not a separate deployable module. A dedicated module would overlap CDP and MA
  without flagship-distinct surface.

### Regulations (q3)

GDPR + CPRA are loaded. The flagship surface implies a broader set, but applicability is
region-dependent. Activity-layer (US, broadly relevant to CRM-ACTIVITY outbound calling +
bulk email): **TCPA, CAN-SPAM**. Privacy regimes (region-dependent): **LGPD** (Brazil),
**PIPEDA** (Canada), **DPDP Act 2023** (India), US state laws **VCDPA / CPA / CTDPA / UCPA**.
Recommend adding TCPA + CAN-SPAM unconditionally (the activity layer touches them regardless of
region) and the privacy regimes per operating region. Several of these `regulations` rows may not
yet exist in the catalog and would be created as part of the load.

## Summary of what changed vs the prior (unvetted) Bucket 3

- The prior audit proposed `sales_forecasts` as a CRM-PIPELINE-MGT master and floated a
  CRM-FORECAST split. Live state shows REV-INTEL already masters forecasting; both are now
  rejected.
- `sales_quotas`, `commission_records`, `partner_relationships` route to SALES-PERF / PRM, which
  are unbuilt stubs -> separate domain builds, not CRM work.
- The genuinely-CRM additive set narrows to 6: competitors, competitor_intelligence,
  lead_assignment_rules, account_hierarchies, sales_playbooks, sales_territories.
- Both module-split candidates are reclassified from b3 to b2 (splits are b2 per Rule #21) and
  recommended for rejection.
