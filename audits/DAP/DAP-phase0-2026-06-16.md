## Compliance entities (non-optional, but light)

DAP is largely unregulated. Two material angles, both real because DAP injects behavioral tracking and UI overlays into apps users did not opt into separately.

| Entity | Regulation | Proposed module |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tracking_consent_records | GDPR Art. 6/7, CCPA/CPRA (behavioral analytics opt-in/opt-out for in-app event capture) | targeting_segmentation |
| data_subject_requests | GDPR DSAR / CCPA deletion (end-user PII captured via user_attributes + events) | targeting_segmentation |
| accessibility_conformance_records | WCAG 2.x / ADA / EN 301 549 (injected overlays must not break host-app a11y; enterprise buyers demand VPAT-level conformance) | guidance_authoring |

`tracking_consent_records` chosen — generic `consent_records` and `gdpr_consent_records` are already mastered/claimed elsewhere; DAP's is tracking-specific. Keep this section honest: vendors expose consent toggles and WCAG statements, not a regulator-filing surface. These three are the floor, not a SOX/HIPAA-style regime.

## Modularization hypothesis

Four modules (Rule #14 satisfied: each has >=1 master; domain has >=4 capabilities so >=2 modules required).

1. **guidance_authoring** (master: walkthroughs) — the in-app overlay surface that vendors build content in: walkthroughs, guided_flows, tooltips, hotspots, launchers, element_selectors, target_applications, content_versions, localizations, themes, task_lists, workflow_automations, plus accessibility_conformance_records. This is the "draw the overlay on the app" core.

2. **engagement_feedback** (master: onboarding_checklists) — proactive/reactive engagement on top of the overlays: checklists + steps, resource_centers + items, in_app_announcements, microsurveys + in_app_survey_responses, nps_scores. The "nudge, help, ask" surface.

3. **targeting_segmentation** (master: user_segments) — who sees what: user_segments, segment_memberships, audience_rules, tracked_users, user_attributes, plus the privacy entities (tracking_consent_records, data_subject_requests). The identity/audience substrate every flow and survey targets.

4. **adoption_analytics** (master: feature_events) — feature_events, tracked_features, usage_funnels, user_paths, retention_cohorts, flow_engagement_stats, goals, session_replays. This is the slice that overlaps a future Product-Analytics domain (see verdicts). Carry it as a DAP module for now, tagged for possible extraction.

If targeting_segmentation proves thin in Phase A, fold it into engagement_feedback; the audience entities sit equally well there.

## Boundary verdicts

For each adjacent domain: what DAP masters vs consumes/embeds/hands-off.

- **ONBOARDING (Employee Onboarding, id 99)** — DAP masters NOTHING that ONBOARDING masters. ONBOARDING owns new-hire journeys/task lists/provisioning. DAP is a delivery layer that overlays "how to use the enterprise app" guidance during the learn-the-tools slice. **Verdict: handoff/consumer, NOT master.** Recommendation: confirm handoff. DAP's `task_lists` are in-app guidance task lists (which overlay steps fire), not HR onboarding task lists; keep the name DAP-scoped and classify ONBOARDING as no-master-overlap with a consumer edge (ONBOARDING can reference DAP flows as enablement content).

- **Adoption-analytics split** — feature_events/funnels/paths/retention/goals are genuinely shared with an unbuilt Product-Analytics domain (Amplitude/Mixpanel), and Pendo proves the fusion is real (it sells DAP and product analytics as one product). **Recommendation: keep `adoption_analytics` IN DAP scope for now as a fourth module, but flag it as the extraction candidate.** When/if a Product-Analytics domain (PA-PROD) is built, the deep event-pipeline/path/cohort engine moves there and DAP retains only `flow_engagement_stats` (per-flow completion/drop-off, which is intrinsically about DAP content) plus consumed funnels. Do not build a standalone analytics domain solely to satisfy DAP; build it when Amplitude/Mixpanel are loaded, then re-home.

- **SOP-MGMT capture/in-app seam** — SOP-MGMT (sibling candidate, unbuilt) masters the published how-to DOCUMENT (system of record). DAP masters the in-app OVERLAY delivery. Scribe/Tango straddle: capture once, publish as a doc OR push as an overlay. **Verdict: DAP masters the overlay artifact (walkthroughs/guided_flows); SOP-MGMT masters the document.** Recommendation: keep DAP to in-app delivery; model a future consumer/handoff edge where a SOP document can be the source content that compiles into a DAP walkthrough. Flag the seam; do not absorb document authoring into DAP.

- **Employee-facing vs customer-facing** — every Core entity (walkthroughs, checklists, surveys, segments, feature_events) is identical in shape regardless of audience; only the buyer/host-app differs (WalkMe/Userlane lean internal-app, Appcues/Userpilot lean product, Whatfix/Pendo do both). **Verdict: this is an AUDIENCE TAG on shared entities, NOT two domains.** Recommendation: add an `audience` enum (customer_facing / employee_facing) on target_applications or on the flow/segment level; do not split DAP into two domains.

Additional cross-domain notes: KMS (knowledge_articles) — DAP's resource_centers reference/embed KMS articles, does not re-master. EMP-EXP — DAP microsurveys are product-feedback (NPS/CES on a feature), classify EMP-EXP as no-master-overlap. LMS — different modality (graded out-of-app courseware), no overlap. DXP/DEM/CSM — clean separation confirmed.
