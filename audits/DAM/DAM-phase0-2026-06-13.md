## Modularization hypothesis (drives B2-1)

The flagship product editions cluster the surface into two consistently-separable workflows:

1. **Asset library** - ingest, store, version, render, taxonomy, collections, rights, model releases. This is the irreducible DAM core; every vendor ships it as the base. `asset_rights` + `model_releases` sit here in brand-led/mid-market products (Bynder, Canto, Frontify) and only split into a separate rights tier in rights-heavy enterprise deployments (Acquia, Aprimo).
2. **Distribution / brand portal** - share links, channel publications, brand portals, usage analytics. Every vendor ships a portal/share surface distinct from the library; Bynder and Frontify make the brand portal the headline.

**Creative review** (review rounds + comments + approval) is a real third surface but is NOT universal as a DAM-native module: Bynder and Aprimo embed it; AEM defers it to Workfront (a separate product); Acquia and Canto treat approval as a lifecycle state on the asset, not a proofing-thread tier. So a dedicated review module is justified only when the proofing-thread entities (review_rounds, review_comments) are confirmed (B3-1), which is post-build additive.

**Rights management as its own module** (4-module split) is justified only in the enterprise rights-heavy tier (Acquia, Aprimo), where license_agreements + asset_rights + model_releases carry their own workflow. Brand-led DAMs keep rights inside the library.

### Verdict per market-shape decision

- **B2-1 (module split): 2-module (Asset Library + Distribution) is the vendor-grounded minimal shape.** Every one of the 5 flagships ships these two surfaces; the library masters the asset + rights + releases + taxonomy, the distribution tier masters share_links + publications. 3-module (add Creative Review) and 4-module (add Rights Mgmt) are real enterprise shapes but each depends on entities not yet confirmed in the catalog (review threads → B3-1; license_agreements → B3-7), so they are additive splits to revisit once those entities land, NOT the initial build. **Recommend (a) 2-module.**
- **B2-2 (PIM seam): keep parallel masters.** PIM's `pim_digital_assets` (product-shot imagery scoped to a SKU) and DAM's `digital_assets` (brand/marketing creative library) are modeled as distinct masters by the vendors too: AEM Assets vs AEM/commerce product media, Acquia DAM vs Acquia PIM are co-deployed parallel stores with a sync, not one merged table. Consolidation is a cross-domain refactor with no vendor demanding it. **Recommend (a) keep-parallel.**
- **B2-3 (creative_briefs/deliverables): embedded_master.** Most brand-led DAMs (Bynder, Canto, Frontify) ship with NO agency tier; the deliverable arrives as an approved asset, not a live read-through to an agency-management master. A local shell keeps DAM deployable standalone. AEM+Workfront shops would prefer consumer, but they are the minority. **Recommend (b) embedded_master.**
- **B2-5 (capability scope): BRAND-MGMT + DIGITAL-RIGHTS-MGMT cross-cutting; rest DAM-prefixed.** Brand management recurs across HCMS (WCO-BRAND-VOICE already exists, id 162), MRM, agency-management; digital rights management recurs across PIM, MRM, agency-management. The storage-specific capabilities (asset-library, taxonomy, brand-portal, distribution, dynamic-delivery) are DAM-only. **Recommend (a).**

## Compliance entities (regulated-market subtraction list, drives B2-4)

- **Copyright / DMCA** - every rights-tracking DAM holds third-party-licensed assets; the dominant DAM compliance anchor. `asset_rights`, `license_agreements` reference it.
- **GDPR / CCPA** - personal imagery (employee photos, talent), model releases as personal-data records. `model_releases`, `has_personal_content` on `digital_assets`.
- **HIPAA** - only healthcare-vertical imagery; situational, defer unless vertical confirmed.
- **Trademark / FTC advertising** - situational (brand portals enforcing usage); defer.

**Recommend B2-4 (a): GDPR + CCPA + Copyright/DMCA now; HIPAA + trademark deferred.**
