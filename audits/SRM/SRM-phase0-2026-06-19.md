# SRM Restructure - Phase 0: Vendor Surface Research

Research only. Zero catalog writes. Evaluates recasting legacy **SUP-LIFE** (domain 28, 6 masters: `suppliers`, `supplier_onboardings`, `supplier_qualifications`, `supplier_certifications`, `supplier_scorecards`, `supplier_risk_assessments`) into a broader **SRM (Supplier Relationship Management)** domain with Supplier Lifecycle Management (SLM) as one module among several.

Neighbors that SRM consumes from or hands off to, never re-masters: **S2P** (id 27, transactional procure-to-pay), **TPRM** (id 19, third-party due diligence, child of GRC), **VMS** (id 64, contingent workforce), **GRC** (id 15).

## Modularization hypothesis (the key deliverable)

The vendor surface clusters cleanly into **three full modules**, each with >=1 master, satisfying Rule #14 (domain with >=3 capabilities needs >=2 full modules; we propose 3).

- **Supplier Lifecycle Management (SLM)** - the master-data + intake + classification spine. Owns the supplier record, its sub-records (contacts, locations, bank accounts, documents, relationships/hierarchy), the onboarding/registration intake funnel, qualification, segmentation, and the information-request/task machinery that keeps data fresh. This is where every vendor's SIM/onboarding product lives. Masters `suppliers` (the domain anchor).
- **Supplier Performance Management (SPM)** - scorecards, KPIs, performance reviews, corrective actions, issues. Cleanly separable: every suite ships it as a distinct module (Ariba Performance, Ivalua SPM, HICX Performance, Jaggaer scorecards).
- **Supplier Risk & Compliance (SRC)** - risk assessments, certifications, and all six compliance entities. This is the module that absorbs the SRM-vs-TPRM tension (see verdict).

## SRM-vs-TPRM boundary verdict

**Verdict: SPLIT, with SRC as a full SRM module.** Operational supplier risk and compliance (qualification-gated risk scoring, certifications, ESG, sanctions screening tied to the supplier master) live in SRM's **Supplier Risk & Compliance** module. Deep, tier-driven third-party due diligence (security questionnaires, breach/ratings ingestion, periodic re-assessment cadence, go/no-go before contract) stays in **TPRM**. SRM consumes TPRM's verdict via handoff; it does not re-master TPRM's questionnaire-cycle territory.

Vendor evidence for the split:
- **apexanalytix** runs both sides itself: its **Discovery** module computes pre-onboarding inherent risk (TPRM-shaped) while its **Registration** + **Bank Account Validation** + **Compliance** modules are supplier-master operational risk (SRM-shaped). Even a single vendor draws the line internally.
- **Ariba SLP** ships supplier risk as an **optional add-on** outside base SLP, signaling that deep risk is a separable concern from lifecycle management, exactly the SRM/TPRM seam.
- **Ivalua** separates **Risk Center** (multi-tier, sub-tier, external intelligence: TPRM-deep) from **SIM/SPM** (operational). The Risk Center is the part that maps to TPRM; SIM-embedded compliance records map to SRM.
- **Graphite** validates against OFAC/LexisNexis and auto-scores at onboarding (SRM-operational screening) but does not run the ongoing tier-cadence due-diligence loop that defines TPRM.

The split is defensible because the SRM compliance entities are **attached to the supplier master and gate qualification** (an SRM job), whereas TPRM's assessments are **engagement/tier-scoped and gate the contract** (a TPRM job). Avoid double-mastering `supplier_risk_assessments`: SRM keeps the qualification-risk view; if TPRM needs its own engagement-risk record it should be a TPRM-owned entity, not a second master on the same object.

## Open questions for the user

1. **Module count: 3 vs 4.** Should `supplier_information_requests` + `supplier_questionnaires` + `supplier_tasks` form a fourth **Supplier Engagement/Collaboration** module (matching Ivalua Supplier Collaboration and HICX Supplier Portal), or stay inside SLM? Vendors are split; default proposal folds them into SLM.
2. **Risk master ownership.** Confirm SRM keeps `supplier_risk_assessments` as its master (qualification-risk) and TPRM does not claim it. If TPRM should own a separate engagement-risk record, name it to avoid a single-master conflict (Rule M7).
3. **Diversity + ESG placement.** Keep `supplier_diversity_classifications` and `supplier_esg_assessments` in SRC, or split sustainability into its own module if the user wants a distinct ESG/sustainable-procurement market entry.
4. **Domain rename + new masters.** Confirm recast of SUP-LIFE -> SRM and the addition of **17 new masters** (3 keep the existing 6). This is a market-shape decision the user must sign off before any Phase-A load.
5. **VMS overlap.** `staffing_suppliers` (VMS) vs `suppliers` (SRM): confirm SRM does not absorb contingent-staffing suppliers (handoff only).
