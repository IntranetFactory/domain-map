# Phase 0 Vendor-Surface Research Report: MRM (Marketing Resource Management)

Date: 2026-06-14
Analyst pass: Phase 0 (vendor-surface, research only, no catalog writes)
Candidate: MRM (Marketing Resource Management) — classic Gartner category covering marketing work, budget, and brand-asset orchestration.

## 0. Live catalog overlap check

Query run against the live catalog (read-only):

```
GET /domains?or=(domain_code.eq.AGENCY-MGMT,domain_code.eq.ADV-AD-TECH,domain_code.eq.DAM,domain_code.eq.MA,domain_code.eq.WORK-MGMT,domain_name.ilike.*marketing*)
```

Returned 5 rows. There is **no MRM domain, no PMM domain, and no ADV-AD-TECH domain** in the catalog today. The adjacent domains that DO exist:

| domain_code | domain_name | crud % | min_org_size | cost_band | Relevance to MRM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MA | Marketing Automation | 75 | 10 xs <50 | $$$ | Email/SMS/journey orchestration and lead nurturing. Execution channel, not work/budget orchestration. Low overlap. |
| DAM | Digital Asset Management | 92 | 20 s <500 | $$$ | Asset storage, taxonomy, rights, distribution. Owns "brand assets". Partial overlap on the asset object only. |
| AGENCY-MGMT | Agency Management Software | 75 | 20 s <500 | $$ | Vertical ops platform for agencies: job/traffic, retainer accounting, media buying, client billing. In-house marketing has no client-billing/commission semantics. Adjacent, not the same buyer. |
| WORK-MGMT | Work Management | 90 | 10 xs <50 | $$ | Horizontal task/project/workflow. Explicitly "bought by ... marketing", but has no marketing-budget-allocation or brand/campaign-calendar semantics. Closest functional overlap on the "work" half of MRM. |

Note ADV-AD-TECH was queried but does not exist in the catalog; it was a candidate metadata adjacency, not a live row.

The decisive question for triage: classic Gartner MRM bundles (a) marketing work/project orchestration, (b) top-down marketing budget allocation and spend tracking, and (c) brand-asset/content workflow. WORK-MGMT covers the work half generically; DAM covers the asset half; neither covers marketing-specific budget allocation. The defensible kernel of MRM is the **marketing planning + budget allocation + spend-to-plan** core, which no existing domain owns.

## 3. Required compliance entities

Minimal. MRM is an internal planning/ops category, not a regulated-data category.

- **GDPR/CCPA personal-data handling:** Not core to MRM itself; lives in MA/CRM where audience data sits. Does not apply.
- **Brand/legal review and rights/usage approval:** A workflow concern, already covered by the review/approval and DAM rights objects (Specialist), not a distinct compliance entity.
- **Financial controls on budget/spend (approval chains, audit trail):** Lightweight governance, modeled as approval workflow on budget lines, not a regulated compliance entity.

Verdict: **No dedicated compliance entities required.**

## 4. Modularization hypothesis

If promoted, MRM splits into two coherent modules plus an optional third:

- **Module A: Marketing Planning & Budget** (the defensible kernel)
  - Entities: marketing_plan, plan_line, campaign/program, marketing_budget, budget_allocation, spend/commitment, vendor/PO (light), forecast/scenario.
- **Module B: Marketing Work & Calendar**
  - Entities: marketing_project/job, task, marketing_calendar, milestone, brief, review/approval, status.
- **Module C (optional): Marketing Performance**
  - Entities: metric, ROI/pipeline-contribution record, plan-to-result link.

Brand-asset storage itself stays in **DAM**; MRM references assets rather than owning the asset repository.

## 5. Point-solution-market test

**Question:** Are there >= 3 INDEPENDENT vendors whose FLAGSHIP product is MRM (specifically the marketing-planning/budget core, not a bolt-on)?

**Answer: Borderline-Yes, on a narrow reading.**

- **Uptempo** — flagship IS marketing planning/budget/spend orchestration. Pure-play MRM. (1)
- **Planful for Marketing (ex-Plannuh)** — a marketing-budget point solution, though now a module of an FP&A platform rather than a standalone company. (2, weakened)
- **Aprimo** — has drifted: its flagship is now positioned as agentic DAM / content lifecycle, with MRM as heritage rather than headline. (borderline)
- **Adobe Workfront**, **Sitecore Content Hub**, **Infor MRM** — all bolt-ons or modules of larger suites (Adobe, Sitecore, Infor ERP), NOT independent flagships.

Only **Uptempo** is unambiguously an independent vendor whose flagship is MRM today. The rest are either modules of bigger platforms (Workfront, Sitecore, Infor, Planful) or have repositioned away from MRM as their headline (Aprimo). The Gartner-observed convergence is real: classic MRM is collapsing into (a) Adobe Workfront + AEM at the top, (b) DAM-led suites (Aprimo, Sitecore, Bynder, Lytho) extending up, and (c) work-management tools extending down. That convergence is precisely what undercuts a clean "3 independent flagships" pass.

## 6. Overlap analysis

**vs WORK-MGMT:** WORK-MGMT covers the marketing-work half (projects, tasks, calendar, approvals) and explicitly names marketing as a buyer. It does NOT cover top-down marketing budget allocation or spend-to-plan reconciliation. So MRM is not fully absorbed by WORK-MGMT, but the work/calendar/approval half of MRM substantially duplicates WORK-MGMT.

**vs DAM:** DAM owns the brand/creative asset repository (storage, taxonomy, rights, distribution) at crud 92. MRM's "brand asset workflow" is a reference into DAM plus review/approval, not a second asset store. Overlap is confined to the asset object and is correctly resolved by MRM referencing DAM.

**vs MA:** Low overlap. MA is channel execution (email/SMS/journeys/nurture). MRM is planning/budget/work upstream of execution. Different layer.

**vs AGENCY-MGMT:** Adjacent, different buyer. AGENCY-MGMT is for agencies (client billing, retainers, media commission). MRM is for in-house marketing departments. No client-billing semantics in MRM.

**The marketing-budget-vs-marketing-work split is the crux.** The "marketing work" half is largely WORK-MGMT (plus DAM for assets). The "marketing budget allocation + spend-to-plan" half is owned by NO existing domain. That budget kernel, with its plan-line hierarchy, top-down allocation, and committed/actual reconciliation, is genuinely distinct from generic work management and from FP&A. It is the only part of MRM that resists folding.

## 7. Triage recommendation

**VERDICT: promote-as-domain** (narrowly scoped to the marketing planning + budget kernel)

**Proposed domain_code:** `MRM`

**Proposed domains.description (one line):**
"Marketing Resource Management: top-down marketing planning, budget allocation across plans and campaigns, spend-to-plan reconciliation, and marketing work/calendar orchestration for in-house marketing departments. References DAM for brand assets and sits upstream of MA execution."

**Rationale (grounded in vendor reality):** A defensible kernel exists, marketing plan + budget allocation + spend-to-plan reconciliation, that no current domain (WORK-MGMT, DAM, MA, AGENCY-MGMT) owns, and at least one independent flagship (Uptempo) plus near-flagships (Planful for Marketing, Aprimo heritage) sell exactly this. The point-solution test passes only narrowly because Gartner-observed convergence is pulling the work/asset halves into WORK-MGMT and DAM-led suites, so MRM should be promoted **scoped tightly to the planning/budget core**, explicitly referencing DAM for assets and not re-implementing generic work management. If a future audit finds the budget kernel too thin to stand alone, the fallback is fold-into-existing:WORK-MGMT with a DAM reference; promotion is the right call now because the budget-orchestration object has no home today.
