## Modularization hypothesis (KEY DELIVERABLE)

The discipline has clearly more than 3 capabilities and multiple distinct masters, so Rule #14 forces >=2 full modules. The vendor surface supports a clean 4-to-5 module CWM. Recommended set: **5 modules.**

**Decision on "VMS": VMS maps to the existing TWO modules, not one.** The existing split (sourcing vs time/invoicing) is real and vendor-validated: Fieldglass, Beeline, and VNDLY all separate the sourcing/assignment surface from the time/invoice/billing surface. So VMS-as-a-discipline becomes the "Staff Augmentation" pillar realized by two modules that already exist, lightly renamed. CWM then adds SOW, Direct Sourcing/FMS, and Classification/Compliance as new modules.

Recommended CWM modules:

1. **VMS / Staff Augmentation** (recast of existing `VMS-WORKER-SOURCING`, 315). Agency-sourced temp labor: requisition -> submission -> assignment -> governed rate cards. Masters: `worker_requisitions`, `candidate_submissions`, `worker_assignments`, `staffing_suppliers`, `rate_cards`, `vendor_scorecards`, `worker_onboardings`, `worker_offboardings`.
2. **Contingent Time & Invoicing** (existing `VMS-TIME-INVOICING`, 316). Masters: `contingent_timesheets`, `contingent_invoices`.
3. **Services Procurement / SOW Management** (NEW). Deliverable/milestone engagements distinct from hourly labor. Masters: `statements_of_work`, `sow_milestones`, `sow_deliverables`, `work_orders` (re-home the stray here if it is SOW/order work; flag for a boundary check vs PM).
4. **Direct Sourcing / Freelance Management (FMS)** (NEW). Agency-less engagement. Masters: `talent_pools`, `freelancer_profiles`. Consumes/links assignments and onboardings.
5. **Worker Classification & Compliance** (NEW, expands existing WORKER-CLASS capability). Masters: `worker_classifications`, `tenure_records`, `compliance_documents`. Consumes `background_checks`.

`contingent_workers` is the shared spine consumed by every module (kept as the domain's identity master, multi-module linked, as it already is across 315/316).

## Boundary verdicts (with vendor evidence)

(a) **staffing_suppliers (CWM) vs suppliers (SRM) - DISTINCT, confirmed.** Fieldglass, Beeline, VNDLY, Prosperix, and Magnit all model the staffing agency as a rate-card-bearing, markup-bearing, tiered-panel entity scored on fill rate and time-to-submit, separate from a procurement supplier master. Magnit explicitly runs supplier panels with tenure/co-employment context. CWM keeps `staffing_suppliers` as its own shape and hands off to SRM `suppliers` for company-level vendor lifecycle; do not merge.

(b) **contingent_workers (CWM) vs employees (HCM) - DISTINCT, confirmed; consume/embed only.** The boundary is worker type: CWM masters non-employees. VNDLY and Worksuite explicitly pair with HCM and create non-employee worker profiles that mirror but do not replace the employee record; VNDLY's value claim is "total workforce visibility when combined with HCM," i.e. two masters joined, not one. CWM may embed worker identity (name, manager, org placement) sourced from HCM but masters only the contingent worker and assignment, never `employees`.

(c) **Worker background checks - CWM CONSUMES, does not master.** Fieldglass, Beeline, VNDLY, Magnit, Prosperix, Worksuite, and WorkMarket all require pre-assignment screening, but treat it as a gating step / consumed result, not a re-mastered FCRA engine. Magnit runs IC screening as a service layer over the check; WorkMarket organizes Labor Clouds by background-check status (a consumed attribute). CWM links to `background_checks` (mastered by ATS today, by a future BGV domain later) and never re-masters the screening machinery.
