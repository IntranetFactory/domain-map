## Compliance entities (non-optional)

CSM is lightly regulated. Only obligations that attach to support-desk data itself apply; the heavyweight regimes belong to sibling domains.

| Entity | Regulation | Proposed module |
| --- | --- | --- |
| consent_records | TCPA (outbound SMS/voice contact), GDPR (lawful-basis for case data) | CSM-CASE-MGMT (112) |
| data_subject_requests | GDPR / CCPA (access, deletion of case correspondence) | CSM-CASE-MGMT (112) — or consume from a central PRIV-MGMT master |
| accessibility_conformance_records | ADA / WCAG (self-service portal + KB) | CSM-KNOWLEDGE (114) — discretionary (b3); rarely a vendor entity |

DSA applies to large EU content platforms, not to a B2B support desk; note but do not load. Prefer consuming `data_subject_requests` from a dedicated privacy master over re-mastering in CSM.

## Modularization hypothesis

The three current modules hold. **CSM-CASE-MGMT (112)** is the execution backbone and absorbs the bulk of the surface: cases, transitions, assignments, escalations, the entire engagement cluster (conversations, comments, CSAT, channels, agent-assist), and all config/templates (routing, automation, canned responses, forms). **CSM-ENTITLEMENTS (113)** masters the contract/SLA substrate (entitlements, service contracts, SLA policies + milestones, assets, business hours). **CSM-KNOWLEDGE (114)** should be **promoted from an editorial view into a first-class master** of a customer-facing knowledge article entity plus categories, versions, community topics, and case-knowledge links (see D1). No fourth module is needed; routing/omnichannel and agent-assist are capabilities inside CASE-MGMT, not their own module.

## Per-decision verdicts

**D1 — Knowledge ownership.** Every flagship masters its OWN customer-facing KB as a first-class product entity: **Zendesk Guide** (Help Center KB, articles + sections + categories, distinct API namespace), **Salesforce Knowledge** (its own `KnowledgeArticleVersion`/`Knowledge__kav` objects, not a borrowed ITSM table), **Freshdesk Solutions** (folders/articles/categories, surfaced by Freddy), **Intercom Articles** (Help Center, fed to Fin AI). **ServiceNow** is the lone case where CSM reuses the platform-wide `kb_knowledge` Knowledge Management table shared with ITSM — but even there it is exposed as a customer-facing service portal KB. **Verdict: 4 of 5 pure/suite CSM vendors master a customer-facing knowledge article in their own right; only ServiceNow shares.** The current catalog modeling — CSM-KNOWLEDGE (114) as a mere "audience-filtered edit" onto ITSM-mastered `knowledge_articles` (id 51, module 43) — under-models the market. CSM-KNOWLEDGE should master a customer-facing knowledge article entity of its own (distinct from internal ITSM KB), not be an editorial view. The ITSM-shared model is a ServiceNow-only artifact, not the market shape.

**D2 — Customer health signals.** No flagship support desk persists a first-class health-score/churn-risk entity. Zendesk/Salesforce/ServiceNow/Freshdesk/Intercom are reactive (manage inbound tickets); customer-health scoring is explicitly **Customer-Success-platform territory** — Gainsight, Totango, ChurnZero master health scores, lifecycle, and SuccessPlays. Support data *feeds into* those scores; it is not mastered by the desk. **Verdict: a CSM-mastered `customer_health_signals` entity is NOT vendor-justified — it is over-modeling.** Keep it Specialist/skip; CSM contributes CSAT/NPS signals (`csat_responses`, already mastered) that a CS platform or CRM consumes. (Note: the existing `csat_responses` description's claim that it "feeds churn-risk signals" is the right boundary — CSM emits signals, it does not master the score.)

**D3 — Capability shape.** Headline capabilities marketed by ALL/most vendors: **case routing/assignment** (all 5: omnichannel + skills-based routing), **omnichannel engagement** (all 5), **agent assist / AI copilot** (all 5: Zendesk Copilot, Agentforce, ServiceNow Now Assist, Freddy, Fin), **CSAT/NPS** (all 5), **knowledge/self-service** (all 5), **SLA management** (4 of 5; absent as a headline only in Intercom). **Verdict: all six warrant first-class CSM capabilities.** Routing, omnichannel, agent-assist, CSAT, and case lifecycle belong to CSM-CASE-MGMT; SLA management and entitlement-bound targets to CSM-ENTITLEMENTS; knowledge/self-service to a promoted CSM-KNOWLEDGE.

**D4 — Inbound consume subjects.**
- `crm_opportunities` (closed-won → onboarding case): **Genuine consume-subject.** Salesforce Service Cloud natively converts post-sale handoffs into onboarding **cases**; the closed-won opportunity becomes the trigger, the work lives as a case. Becomes a case.
- `support_sessions` (CCaaS/voice session escalating): **Genuine consume-subject.** Salesforce Agentforce Contact Center and Zendesk voice make a voice/contact-center session natively escalate into a case with full transcript context. Becomes a case (transcript → case_conversation on that case).
- product-feedback signals (`product_features`/`product_releases`/`beta_programs`/`customer_feedback_items`/`product_metrics`): **Largely NOT a CSM master; thin consume only.** Vendors capture feedback as case tags/CSAT free-text or route it out to a product/feedback tool (Productboard-class). At most it lands as an **activity/comment on a case**, not a first-class CSM entity. Verdict: consume as case context, do not master a product-feedback entity in CSM.
