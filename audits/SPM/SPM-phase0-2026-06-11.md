# SPM Phase 0 — Vendor surface research (2026-06-11)

Purpose: ground the SPM-vs-SEM boundary decision (state.yaml B2-S1 / q-SPM.md q1) in named-vendor evidence, per SKILL.md Rule #22. The prior audit recommended "keep split" but on a forbidden rationale ("least disruptive / matches live state"). This report re-grounds the call in the actual vendor market.

## The two candidate markets

### SPM market (Gartner Magic Quadrant for Strategic Portfolio Management, 2025)
- Flagship vendors: ServiceNow SPM, Planview Portfolios, Broadcom Clarity, Planisware, Bizzdesign/Alfabet, Shibumi, Cora Systems.
- Buyer: PMO / EPMO / CIO / IT-led transformation office.
- US TAM (catalog): ~$2,500M.
- Gartner definition: "a set of business capabilities, processes and supporting portfolio management technology to create a portfolio of strategic options that focus an organization's finite resources to execute the enterprise-wide business strategy."
- Surfaces: demand intake, business-value scoring, portfolio structuring, roadmaps, resource & capacity management, scenario / what-if planning, benefits realization. (ServiceNow's "Strategic Planning Workspace" ADDS goals/OKRs + scenario planning on top of the core "Portfolio Planning Workspace".)

### Strategy-execution / OKR market (the SEM domain)
- Flagship vendors (catalog SEM roster): Cascade, ClearPoint Strategy, AchieveIt, Quantive Results, WorkBoard, Profit.co, i-nexus, Spider Impact, Envisio, Microsoft Viva Goals; secondary HR-adjacent: Lattice, 15Five, Leapsome.
- Buyer: Chief Strategy Officer / Chief of Staff / Strategy Office.
- US TAM (catalog): ~$600M (≈4x smaller than SPM).
- Surfaces: strategic objective definition, strategy-map visualization, OKR/goal management, strategic-initiative tracking, operating cadence (QBR/MBR), strategy-to-execution traceability.

## Key evidence

1. **Gartner treats Strategy Execution Management as a *use case within* the SPM category** (one of three: Strategy Execution Management, Enterprise Program & Portfolio Management, Integrated IT Portfolio Analysis). So at the suite level it is NOT a separate Gartner market — the SPM heavyweights are expanding to cover it.

2. **But a distinct pure-play vendor market for strategy execution / OKRs persists.** Cascade, ClearPoint, AchieveIt, Quantive, WorkBoard, Profit.co, i-nexus are companies whose *entire* flagship product is strategy execution / OKRs — none competes in the Gartner SPM MQ. That is ≥3 independent vendors whose flagship IS this surface → it passes the point-solution-market test on its own. Different buyer, ~4x smaller TAM.

3. **Market is consolidating but not collapsed.** WorkBoard acquired Quantive (Gtmhub) in May 2025; the SPM suites (ServiceNow, Planview) keep absorbing OKRs. This is the classic umbrella-overlap shape: big suites expand into an adjacent pure-play market that still exists independently.

4. **`strategic_initiatives` is the genuine overlap entity.** Both markets model it: ServiceNow manages "initiatives / epics / strategic programs" in its portfolio-planning workspace; the pure-play execution tools track initiatives as the work under objectives. The catalog currently masters it in SEM (module SEM-EXECUTION-TRACKING, id 106) with a full lifecycle (proposed → approved → in_progress → completed/cancelled). Single-master rule ⇒ only one domain can master it.

## Verdict per decision

- **B2-S1 boundary → Recommend (c) keep the split**, on vendor grounds: SEM is anchored in a distinct pure-play vendor roster (Cascade/WorkBoard/Quantive/ClearPoint/Profit.co/Viva Goals) with a different buyer and ~4x smaller TAM. SPM owns the planning/investment/scenario/capacity/benefits surface (8 unique masters); SEM keeps execution/OKR/operating-rhythm. The overlap is handled by the shared dual-linked capability (SEM-PORTFOLIO-ALIGN) and by single-mastership.
  - **Counter-case (b) merge SEM into SPM**: defensible if you weight the suite-level consolidation (Gartner "use case within SPM"; ServiceNow/Planview absorbing OKRs) over the pure-play market. Cost: re-homes SEM's 3 built modules and discards the distinct $600M strategy-execution vendor market as a separate entry. Not recommended — it collapses two real markets.
  - **(a) merge SPM into SEM**: rejected. SPM is the larger, Gartner-named umbrella market; subordinating it to SEM inverts the category hierarchy.

- **`strategic_initiatives` (274) mastery → stays with SEM; SPM embeds (`embedded_master + required`)**. Both markets model it, but the catalog already masters it in SEM with a full lifecycle wired into SEM-EXECUTION-TRACKING. Moving mastery to SPM would orphan that built lifecycle for no vendor-driven payoff. The embedded_master shell makes SPM deployable standalone (the shell IS the initiatives table when SEM is absent) and demotes to consumer when SEM co-deploys.

## Module hypothesis for SPM under (c)

8 SPM masters → 3 full modules (well above the ≥2 floor for a ≥3-capability domain):
- **SPM-DEMAND-MGMT** — masters: demand_intake_requests (278), business_value_assessments (276). Demand intake + value scoring/prioritization.
- **SPM-PORTFOLIO-PLANNING** — masters: strategic_portfolios (273), roadmap_items (275), dependency_chains (280), benefits_tracking_records (281); embedded_master: strategic_initiatives (274 from SEM). Portfolio structuring, roadmaps, dependency mapping, benefits realization.
- **SPM-RESOURCE-CAPACITY** — masters: resource_allocations (277), scenario_plans (279); consumers: workforce_scenarios (27 SWP), financial_scenarios (40 EPM), position_demand_forecasts (25 SWP, contributor), work_items (243 WORK-MGMT), service_projects (216 PSA). Resource allocation + capacity what-if.
