# SCRM (Supply Chain Risk Management) - Phase 0: Vendor Surface Research

Research only. Zero catalog writes. Grounds the build of a NEW domain SCRM, promoted from the
missing-domains queue on 2026-06-19 (SRM audit b2 item B2-S4, user chose "promote as its own
new domain"). SCRM is the supply-chain-disruption-intelligence overlay: N-tier / sub-tier
supplier mapping plus continuous monitoring of EXTERNAL disruption signals across suppliers the
buyer often has no direct relationship with.

Basis: analyst flagship-vendor knowledge (cutoff Jan 2026), consistent with the named-vendor
evidence already captured in the SRM audit's B2-S4. Neighbors SCRM consumes from / hands off to,
never re-masters: **SRM** (id 28, supplier master + operational risk), **TPRM** (id 19, direct
third-party diligence, child of GRC), **GRC** (id 15, enterprise risk register), **S2P** (id 27,
sourcing / procure-to-pay), **ESG** (id 21, supplier ESG scoring).

## Modularization hypothesis (the key deliverable)

The vendor surface clusters into **three full modules**:

- **SCRM-NETWORK-MAPPING** - the N-tier / sub-tier map: nodes, links, facilities, sub-tier discoveries. Embeds SRM `suppliers`. This is the part Resilinc (BOM/part-site map), Interos (relationship graph), Sayari (Sayari Map), and Everstream (multi-tier visibility) all sell.
- **SCRM-RISK-INTELLIGENCE** - continuous monitoring + scoring: monitored entities, risk events, alerts, risk scores, indicators, plus corporate-ownership and forced-labor exposure. Every vendor's monitoring engine (EventWatchAI, Interos models, Everstream predictive scores, Prewave AI alerts, Sphera risk radar).
- **SCRM-DISRUPTION-RESPONSE** - act on disruptions: incidents, impact / revenue-at-risk assessments, mitigation actions. Resilinc WhatIf, Everstream exposure, Sphera response.

## SCRM-vs-neighbors boundary verdict

- **vs SRM (28):** SCRM consumes SRM `suppliers` (embedded_master) but masters the network graph + risk events + network risk scores. SRM consumes SCRM's elevated risk via handoff (`supply_chain_risk.elevated` -> SRM supplier risk view). No double-master: SRM keeps qualification-risk on its own supplier master; SCRM keeps disruption-risk on the N-tier graph.
- **vs TPRM (19):** TPRM = engagement / contract-gated deep diligence on DIRECT third parties (questionnaire cadence). SCRM = continuous EXTERNAL monitoring across INDIRECT / sub-tier suppliers. Distinct; no shared master.
- **vs GRC (15):** GRC consumes elevated supply-chain risk into the enterprise risk register (handoff).
- **vs ESG (21):** ESG masters `supplier_esg_assessments` (the scorecard). SCRM monitors ESG / forced-labor disruption SIGNALS as events. Decision point: does SCRM master `forced_labor_exposures` as its own event-shaped record, or consume ESG? (Pure-plays Prewave / Sayari / SupplyShift treat forced-labor as a monitored signal, arguing SCRM masters the exposure record.)
- **vs S2P (27):** S2P consumes SCRM risk into sourcing/award decisions (handoff).

## Open questions for the user (-> q-SCRM.md)

1. **Module split: 3 vs 2.** Three modules (NETWORK-MAPPING / RISK-INTELLIGENCE / DISRUPTION-RESPONSE) vs folding DISRUPTION-RESPONSE into RISK-INTELLIGENCE (2 modules).
2. **Node identity.** Does SCRM master its own `supply_chain_nodes` (incl. indirect / sub-tier entities with no SRM record) AND embed SRM `suppliers`, or only consume SRM `suppliers` (no separate node master)?
3. **Forced-labor / ESG scope.** Does SCRM master `forced_labor_exposures` (event-shaped exposure) or defer entirely to the ESG domain?
4. **Owner function.** Supply Chain vs Procurement vs GRC as the owning business function.
