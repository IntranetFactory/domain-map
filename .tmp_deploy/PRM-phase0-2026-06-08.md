# PRM Phase 0 — Vendor Surface (2026-06-08)

Domain: Partner Relationship Management (domain id 96, parent CRM id 69). Currently UNBUILT (0 modules / 0 capabilities / 0 masters / 0 solutions), record_status `new`. There are no existing TCMA, ECOSYSTEM-LED-GROWTH, or AFFILIATE-MGMT domains in the catalog yet, so any relocation target proposed below would be a new sibling domain, not an existing one.

Research basis: current (2025-2026) vendor product/feature pages, developer/API docs, pricing pages, and analyst positioning. Two analyst signals frame the whole question:

- Gartner renamed the category in September 2025 from "Partner Relationship Management" to "Partner and Ecosystem Relationship Management (PERM)" (Gartner Market Guide, 2025-09-23): the analyst view is that classic PRM is absorbing co-sell / ecosystem motions, not that they are one product today.
- Forrester keeps three SEPARATE Waves/Landscapes: "PRM Platforms" (Q4 2025), "Partner Marketing Automation Platforms" i.e. TCMA (Q2 2025, where StructuredWeb and ZINFI are named Leaders), and ecosystem/account-mapping as its own motion. Forrester treats TCMA and PRM as adjacent-but-distinct markets.

## Carve-out verdicts (the decisions this feeds)

### A. TCMA — distinct domain or PRM module?

VERDICT: distinct domain (promote TCMA out of PRM), with a thin shared MDF/co-op fund boundary.

Named-vendor evidence:
- Impartner states on its own PRM page that "Through Channel Marketing Automation (TCMA) ... May be purchased without PRM", and sells "Partner Marketing Automation (PMA)" as a distinct solution module. So even the leader sells TCMA as a separately-licensed SKU, not an inseparable PRM feature.
- Zift markets PRM + TCMA + C-LMS "built as one to work as one" in one platform: a bundle, but the company still names TCMA as a discrete capability set.
- ZINFI splits the work across its 24 modules: PRM-type modules (Programs, Contracts) are separate from the channel-marketing modules (Email, Social, Microsites, Events).
- The TCMA pure-plays target a DIFFERENT buyer. SproutLoud sells "brand-to-local" distributed marketing to channel-marketing managers (co-op claims, asset distribution, local campaign execution, instant funding) with no deal-registration or partner-tiering surface. Structured (StructuredWeb) is a content-syndication / demand-gen tool and was named a Leader in Forrester's Partner Marketing Automation Platforms Wave Q2 2025, a market Forrester scores separately from PRM Platforms (Q4 2025). Mindmatrix is the blur case (ships both), but it positions as "Ecosystem Orchestration" partner marketing, not deal-reg PRM.

The buyer split (channel-chief/channel-ops for PRM vs channel-marketing-manager for TCMA) plus the separate-SKU reality plus the separate Forrester Wave make TCMA its own domain. The only genuinely shared concept is MDF / co-op funds (Impartner, Channeltivity, SproutLoud, ZINFI all master fund requests/claims). Recommendation: MDF/co-op fund accrual and claims stay in PRM as the system of record for partner financial commitment; campaign execution, asset syndication, and co-branded content generation relocate to TCMA.

### B. Ecosystem-led / co-sell — distinct domain or PRM module?

VERDICT: distinct domain (promote ECOSYSTEM-LED-GROWTH), with co-sell SIGNALS surfaced inside PRM by integration.

Named-vendor evidence:
- Crossbeam and Reveal MERGED in 2024 into a single company (Crossbeam) whose entire product is cross-company account mapping / overlap detection / Ecosystem Qualified Leads. None of it does deal registration, MDF, partner tiering, or partner training. It is a data-network category, not a PRM.
- PartnerTap explicitly "works alongside traditional PRM platforms rather than replacing them" and pushes mapped accounts into PRM "with native integrations to Impartner and Salesforce." That is the definition of an adjacent system PRM integrates with.
- The classic PRM vendors do NOT ship cross-company account mapping natively. Salesforce Partner Cloud's PEM tier ($50/member) adds co-sell alerts and strategic account planning, and Salesforce sells "Ecosystem-Led Growth" as a separate AppExchange listing rather than baking a partner-data-network into PRM. Oracle PRM offers internal lead sharing and deal registration, not cross-org overlap mapping.

The distinguishing entity is the account-overlap / data-share-population concept, which requires a second company's CRM and a privacy-preserving sharing model. That cannot live inside a single tenant's PRM. Recommendation: account mapping, overlaps, data-share populations, and partner-sourced/influenced pipeline detection relocate to ECOSYSTEM-LED-GROWTH; PRM keeps a lightweight "co-sell signal" / partner-influenced-opportunity record that the ecosystem domain feeds.

### C. Affiliate — distinct domain?

VERDICT: distinct domain (AFFILIATE-MGMT). PartnerStack sits on the boundary and argues for a shared partner/commission master, not for merging the two.

Named-vendor evidence:
- The affiliate entity surface (Everflow developer docs, Impact.com, Refersion, CJ/Awin/Rakuten) is built around offers, tracking links / deep links, clicks, conversions with attribution windows and cookie/coupon durations, creatives, data feeds, conversion-journey attribution, and scheduled performance payouts with tax forms. None of that exists in classic-PRM docs (no deal registration, MDF, partner tiering, QBR, or training).
- The buyer is a DTC performance-marketing manager, not a channel chief. CJ, Awin, and Rakuten are two-sided publisher/advertiser networks, structurally unlike a single-vendor reseller portal.
- PartnerStack is the crossover: it unifies affiliate + referral + reseller + co-sell on one hub with native commissions/payouts. But it reaches that unification by treating "affiliate", "reseller", and "referral" as different partner TYPES sharing a common partner + commission + payout spine, not by making affiliate tracking a PRM portal feature. ZINFI similarly keeps Affiliate (the Referral module) as a distinct module within UPM.

So affiliate is its own domain (tracking, conversions, performance payouts, creatives, data feeds). PartnerStack's lesson is that `partner_accounts`, `commission_structures`, `commission_accruals`, and `partner_payouts` are concepts SHARED across PRM and affiliate, candidates for a future partner/commission master, rather than evidence that affiliate belongs inside PRM.

### D. Partner training & certification ownership

VERDICT: PRM MASTERS the enablement transactions (enrollments, certifications, training-credit records) and CONSUMES external course content via SCORM. Training/certification stays in PRM as a module; it does not need to relocate to a standalone LMS domain.

Named-vendor evidence:
- Impartner ships a native "Partner Training & Certification" module ("SCORM-enabled courses, quizzes & progress tracking", ROI analysis) and in April 2025 added SCORM ingestion so customers can bring existing course content. So Impartner masters enrollments/certifications/competencies but consumes authored course packages.
- Channeltivity released a native Training & Certification module (courses, certifications, honors in-person training credits, distributes certificates, posts LinkedIn digital badges).
- Allbound and Magentrix both ship native learning management / certification.
- Zift bundles Channel Learning Management (C-LMS) directly into ZiftONE.
- Enterprise LMSs (Docebo, Litmos) exist as external partner-training options, which is why SCORM ingestion matters: the PRM is the enrollment/certification system of record, the LMS is an optional content source.

Entities PRM masters: course catalog entries, course enrollments, certifications, certification renewals, training-credit records, competency/badge records. Entity PRM consumes: external SCORM course packages (referenced, not authored).

### E. Partner-channel commission ownership

VERDICT: PRM MASTERS channel-incentive accrual and payout (commission, rebate, SPIF, MDF settlement). It does NOT hand this to a sales-comp system; sales-rep direct compensation is a different domain with a different buyer.

Named-vendor evidence:
- ZINFI's INCENTIVIZE zone consolidates four applications, Commissions, MDF, Rebates, and Payment, and the Payment Management application is described as the "Single Source of Truth (SSoT) for partner financial data" disbursing commissions, referral bonuses, MDF claims, and rebates. ZINFI masters the full partner-financial lifecycle inside the PRM platform.
- Impartner natively automates commissions, rebates, and bonuses with rule-based calculation, point-based rewards tied to deals/training/milestones, real-time dashboards, and approval workflows, then syncs reward data to CRM/external systems for fulfillment.
- Salesforce splits the two motions on purpose: Partner Cloud's Channel Revenue Management (rebates, channel pricing, inventory) handles the CHANNEL side, while Salesforce Spiff (Incentive Compensation Management) handles DIRECT sales-rep comp. The rebate/partner-incentive side lives with PRM; rep comp does not.

Entities PRM masters: commission structures/rules, commission accruals, rebate programs, rebate accruals, SPIF/bonus programs, partner payout records, payout statements. PRM hands off: only the final cash disbursement to an external payments rail (and rep-level sales compensation, which is out of scope entirely).

## Modularization hypothesis

Rule #14 applied throughout: every module has >=1 master; a sub-market with >=3 capabilities gets >=2 modules. Funds (MDF/co-op) and channel incentives stay in PRM under every scenario (verdicts A-boundary and E). Training stays in PRM under every scenario (verdict D).

### Scenario 1 — 4 modules (cleanest classic-PRM-only; RECOMMENDED)

Relocate TCMA, ecosystem-led growth, and affiliate to three new sibling domains. PRM keeps only what every classic-PRM leader masters plus the financial spine and enablement.

1. partner_management — partner_accounts, partner_contacts, partner_types, partner_tiers, partner_programs, partner_contracts, partner_onboarding_applications, partner_portal_pages, partner_journeys, partner_announcements, partner_compliance_records, tier_compliance_checks, partner_due_diligence_records, anti_corruption_attestations, partner_data_processing_consents (master: partner_accounts).
2. deal_and_pipeline — deal_registrations, deal_registration_approvals, deal_conflict_resolutions, partner_leads, registered_opportunities, partner_referrals, partner_influenced_opportunities (master: deal_registrations).
3. funds_and_incentives — mdf_funds, mdf_requests, mdf_claims, coop_fund_accruals, proof_of_spend_records, commission_structures, commission_accruals, rebate_programs, rebate_accruals, spif_programs, partner_payouts, payout_statements, mdf_spend_audit_records, commission_accrual_audit_trails (master: mdf_funds; commission_structures as second master if split).
4. enablement_and_performance — course_enrollments, certifications, training_credits, partner_playbooks, joint_business_plans, quarterly_business_reviews, partner_scorecards (master: certifications / joint_business_plans).

Relocated: TCMA domain (co_branded_collateral, through_channel_campaigns, content_syndications, local_marketing_executions; consumes PRM mdf_claims); ECOSYSTEM-LED-GROWTH domain (account_mappings, account_overlaps, data_share_populations, ecosystem_qualified_leads, cosell_signals; feeds PRM partner_influenced_opportunities); AFFILIATE-MGMT domain (affiliate_accounts, affiliate_offers, tracking_links, coupon_codes, affiliate_clicks, affiliate_conversions, attribution_rules, affiliate_creatives, product_data_feeds, performance_contracts, affiliate_tax_forms).

### Scenario 2 — 5 modules (keep TCMA inside PRM)

As Scenario 1, but add a 5th module `through_channel_marketing` (co_branded_collateral, through_channel_campaigns, content_syndications, local_marketing_executions; consumes funds_and_incentives MDF). Justified by Zift/Mindmatrix bundling. Ecosystem and affiliate still relocate. Choose this only if the build wants the Zift "all-in-one" posture rather than the Impartner separate-SKU posture.

### Scenario 3 — 6 modules (keep TCMA + ecosystem-led)

Scenario 2 plus a 6th module `ecosystem_and_cosell` (account_mappings, account_overlaps, data_share_populations, ecosystem_qualified_leads, cosell_signals, partner_influenced_opportunities). This is the Gartner PERM posture (PRM absorbing ecosystem). Affiliate still relocates.

### Scenario 4 — 7 modules (keep everything, PartnerStack/ZINFI maximalist)

Scenario 3 plus a 7th module `affiliate_partnerships` (affiliate_accounts, affiliate_offers, tracking_links, coupon_codes, affiliate_clicks, affiliate_conversions, attribution_rules, affiliate_creatives, product_data_feeds, performance_contracts, affiliate_tax_forms). Mirrors PartnerStack/ZINFI "one platform, all partner types." Risk: the affiliate buyer (DTC performance marketer) and entity surface (clicks/conversions/attribution) are genuinely foreign to channel-chief PRM, and CJ/Awin/Rakuten/Impact/Everflow/Refersion are a self-contained market. Keep-everything maximizes surface but blurs the buyer story most.

Recommended: Scenario 1 (4 modules), promoting TCMA, ECOSYSTEM-LED-GROWTH, and AFFILIATE-MGMT to sibling domains under CRM, with MDF/co-op funds and channel incentives retained in PRM as the financial system of record and training/certification retained as a PRM enablement module.
