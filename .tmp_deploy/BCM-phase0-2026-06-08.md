## Compliance entities (non-optional for the regulated slice)

BCM today carries four mandatory regulations: ISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF, NIS2, DORA. The compliance surface the market expects:

| Entity | Regulation | Proposed module / owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `regulatory_continuity_reports` | DORA, NIS2 (continuity / resilience reporting) | BCM-PLANNING (BCM-mastered) |
| `dora_third_party_register_entries` (ICT Register of Information) | DORA Art. 28 | TPRM-mastered, BCM consumes (see B2-3) |
| `ict_third_party_incidents` | DORA major-incident reporting | TPRM / SECOPS adjacency; BCM consumes |
| TLPT / threat-led test artifacts | DORA Art. 26 | SECOPS / pen-test adjacency, not BCM-mastered |

Note: vendors do not all surface these as public schema objects, but DORA mandates the register and the reporting regardless of vendor presence. Fusion explicitly routes the ICT Register of Information through its **TPRM offering**, not its BCM module: "Fusion ... has a robust TPRM offering" and the Register of Information is a third-party-contract artifact. This is the named-vendor basis for B2-3 = "register lives in TPRM, BCM consumes".

## Modularization hypothesis

Two full modules cleanly cover the surface, matching how the pure-plays decompose:

- **BCM-PLANNING** masters the planning substrate: `business_impact_analyses`, `business_continuity_plans`, `disaster_recovery_plans`, `recovery_strategies`, `dependency_maps`, plus `business_services` as embedded_master from CMDB and the plan-version / review-cycle currency junctions. This is the "know what would hurt, plan the recovery" surface every vendor builds first (Fusion's Business Function + plan tabs; ServiceNow's BIA -> plan flow; Castellan's integrated BIA + plan development).
- **BCM-EXERCISE-AND-CRISIS** masters the test-and-respond substrate: `continuity_exercises`, `exercise_findings`, `crisis_events`, `crisis_communications`, and `emergency_contacts` (embedded_master from users). This is the "prove the plan works, then run it for real" surface (Fusion plan exercising + crisis/incident mgmt; ServiceNow recovery exercises + crisis management; Castellan testing/exercising + crisis/incident).

A third optional module (BCM-DORA-RESILIENCE) is NOT justified by the BCM vendor surface: the DORA ICT register is a TPRM artifact (Fusion routes it through TPRM), and continuity reporting folds into BCM-PLANNING. Promote a separate module only if the user wants a DORA-scoped marketing surface (B2-3 option c).

## Decision-grounding summary (drives the q-file)

1. **Promote vs leadership-tier (B2-1).** Two flagship pure-plays (Fusion, Castellan) plus three suites (ServiceNow, Archer, MetricStream) each master first-class BCM records (BIA, BCP, exercises, crisis events, dependency maps). That is the Rule #2 point-solution-market test. BCM is master-bearing, not a derive/overlay. -> Promote (b). No reversal: confirms the prior recommendation with named-vendor surface evidence.

2. **DR/DRP placement (B2-2).** Fusion, Riskonnect, and ServiceNow all fold IT disaster recovery INTO the resilience platform as an integrated component, not a separate standalone module/domain. Archer keeps distinct BC-plan and DR-plan TYPES but under ONE "Business Continuity & IT Disaster Recovery Planning" use case. No flagship sells DRP as a domain independent of BCM. -> Keep DR inside BCM as `disaster_recovery_plans` + `recovery_strategies` on BCM-PLANNING (a). No reversal; the fresh evidence strengthens it.

3. **DORA ICT third-party register ownership (B2-3).** Fusion explicitly routes the Register of Information through its TPRM offering, not its BCM module. The register is fundamentally a third-party-contract artifact. -> TPRM masters it, BCM consumes (b). No reversal.

4. **Mass-notification boundary (framing).** Everbridge inverts the stack: critical-event-management + mass-notification is the platform, and BCM (BC in the Cloud) is the acquired module. The BCM pure-plays embed emergency notification as a FEATURE (MetricStream 25+ channels; Castellan emergency notification). -> BCM masters `crisis_communications` + `emergency_contacts` (the log and roster), but heavyweight mass-notification DISPATCH is a tool/integration (notify_team), not a BCM master. No split of a mass-notification sub-market out of BCM is warranted; the existing q-file does not propose one, so no change.

Net: zero recommendations reversed. All three market-shape calls (promote, DR-inside-BCM, register-in-TPRM) are confirmed by fresh named-vendor evidence and re-grounded inline.
