# ELM Phase 0 — Vendor Surface (Legal Entity Management)

Research only, no inserts. Backs the Corporate Legal agent (Entity Compliance Tracker).

## Catalog reconciliation (checked live)

- `legal_entities` (data_object **197**) already exists, **mastered by FIN — Core Financial Management (domain 65)**, consumed by PAYROLL (55) and BEN-ADMIN (61). Its description is accounting-flavored: "Statutory company: legal name, registration, jurisdiction, currency, fiscal calendar. Books are kept per legal entity; consolidation rolls them up."
- **Verdict: ELM should CONSUME `legal_entities` (197), not re-master it.** FIN owns the accounting identity (books, currency, fiscal calendar, consolidation rollup). ELM masters the *governance/statutory* facts hung off that same entity: registrations, agents, filings, officers, ownership. Re-mastering would fork the corporate register. ELM masters everything *around* 197 and holds a required-consumer edge to it. (If FIN's 197 proves too thin for governance, the follow-up is to *enrich* 197 in place, still FIN-mastered, not to duplicate.)
- **CAP-TABLE = domain 162** (Cap Table Management & Equity Administration): shareholder ledger, option pools, 409A, ASC 718. Distinct from ELM (see boundaries).
- No BOARD-MGMT domain exists in the catalog yet; treated as a hypothetical sibling below.

## Modularization hypothesis (Rule #14)

Three modules, each with >=1 master; ELM has >=3 capabilities so >=2 modules is satisfied:

1. **entity_register** — the corporate register: the entity itself (consuming FIN's `legal_entities` 197), `entity_types`, `jurisdictions`, `entity_registrations`, `registered_agents`, `registered_addresses`, `entity_documents`/`document_versions`, `entity_notes`. Masters: `entity_registrations`, `registered_agents`, `entity_documents`. This is "who exists, where, and with what papers."

2. **statutory_compliance** (statutory filings & compliance calendar) — `statutory_filings`, `annual_reports`, `business_licenses`, `tax_registrations`, `compliance_deadlines`/`compliance_tasks`/`compliance_events`, `good_standing_certificates`, `beneficial_ownership_reports`, `beneficial_owners`, `service_of_process_records`, `filing_templates`, `jurisdiction_requirements`, `compliance_notifications`. Masters: `statutory_filings`, `compliance_deadlines`. The obligation-and-deadline engine; carries all Compliance-class entities. This is the module that most directly powers the Entity Compliance Tracker agent.

3. **governance_structure** (ownership & governance) — `entity_officers`, `entity_directors`, `authorized_signatories`, `ownership_interests`, `ownership_structures`/`org_charts`, `entity_relationships`, `resolutions`, `minute_books`, `share_classes`. Masters: `ownership_interests`, `entity_officers`. The people-and-ownership graph: who controls/owns/signs, and the parent-subsidiary org chart.

If `share_classes`/`shares_issued` prove thin (they overlap CAP-TABLE), collapse them into `ownership_interests` rather than a fourth module.

## Boundary reads

- **ELM ↔ FIN (`legal_entities` 197):** FIN masters the accounting identity of the entity (books, currency, fiscal calendar, consolidation). ELM consumes 197 and masters the governance/statutory layer around it (registrations, agents, filings, officers, ownership). One entity, two masters of *different* facts. ELM does **not** re-create `legal_entities`.

- **ELM ↔ CAP-TABLE (domain 162):** CAP-TABLE owns the **shareholder ledger / equity mechanics** — share issuances, option pools, RSU/option grant workflows, 409A, exit waterfalls, ASC 718 comp accounting. ELM's `ownership_interests` is the **corporate-control / UBO** view: which entity owns what percentage of which subsidiary, for org-chart and beneficial-ownership purposes. The line: CAP-TABLE = *equity holders of a single company for financing/comp*; ELM = *ownership edges between entities in a corporate group for governance/compliance*. `share_classes`/`shares_issued` sit on the CAP-TABLE side; if ELM needs them it consumes, it does not master. Individual grant/vesting rows are firmly CAP-TABLE.

- **ELM ↔ BOARD-MGMT (hypothetical sibling):** Board/committee **meeting operations** — agendas, board packs, meeting minutes, voting, board portals, director communications — belong to BOARD-MGMT. ELM masters the **statutory roster**: who the officers/directors *are* (appointment/resignation of record, signatory authority) as a filed corporate fact. The line: ELM = *the register of officers as a compliance fact*; BOARD-MGMT = *the workflow of the board doing its work*. `resolutions`/`minute_books` are a genuine overlap: the statutory minute book (a required corporate record) leans ELM; the live meeting-and-vote workflow leans BOARD-MGMT. Until BOARD-MGMT exists, ELM may hold `resolutions`/`minute_books` as the statutory record and hand them off later.
