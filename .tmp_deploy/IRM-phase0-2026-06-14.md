**Date:** 2026-06-14
**Candidate (queued):** Insider Risk Management, queue code `IRM`
**Chosen domain_code:** `INSIDER-RISK` (see Code-collision section; `IRM` is overloaded)
**Verdict:** `promote-as-domain`

---

## Code-collision decision (IRM)

`IRM` is an overloaded acronym. In enterprise software it most commonly means **Integrated Risk Management** (the GRC rebrand, ServiceNow IRM).

Live catalog check (`/domains` query):
- **No domain is literally coded `IRM`**: the code string is free.
- BUT domain **`GRC` (id 15, "Governance, Risk and Compliance")** already carries the description "**Integrated risk management** spanning policy, controls, risk assessment, and compliance evidence." The Integrated-Risk-Management meaning is therefore already occupied conceptually by GRC.

Reusing `IRM` for *Insider* Risk Management would collide semantically with GRC and confuse buyers. **Chosen code: `INSIDER-RISK`** (non-colliding, unambiguous). System skill is `insider-risk-system`.

**Is insider risk a distinct market or a fold-in?** Distinct. DLP enforces content egress at the perimeter (one signal). DSPM discovers data at rest. UEBA scores anomalies generically. Insider Risk Management is the **case-and-investigation discipline** that fuses behavioral risk scoring, departing-employee monitoring, file-lineage forensics, and HR/Legal evidence handoff into a human-centric investigation workflow. It consumes DLP/DSPM/UEBA signals but masters its own nouns (cases, alerts, user risk scores, investigation evidence, exit risk assessments). Gartner publishes a **dedicated Market Guide for Insider Risk Management Solutions** (March 12, 2025) and a dedicated Peer Insights market, strong recognition of a standalone market.

## Point-solution-market test

**Threshold:** at least 3 INDEPENDENT vendors whose FLAGSHIP product is insider risk / insider threat.

**PASS.** At least three dedicated pure-plays:
1. **DTEX InTERCEPT**: flagship IS insider risk management.
2. **Teramind**: flagship IS insider threat / employee monitoring.
3. **Proofpoint ITM**: dedicated insider threat management product line (ex-ObserveIT).

Plus Code42/Mimecast Incydr (departing-user / data-exfil focus) and Microsoft Purview IRM (suite). Many DLP/UEBA vendors carry it as a module, but the existence of at least 3 dedicated flagships plus a Gartner Market Guide plus a Peer Insights market confirms a standalone point-solution market, not merely a DLP/UEBA feature.

## Compliance entities (market-driven)

Insider Risk operates under employee-monitoring privacy law (GDPR/works-council in EU, state surveillance law in US). `monitoring_consent_records` and `legal_hold_records` are relevant; for v1 they are represented via case/evidence governance fields and an outbound Legal handoff rather than a dedicated compliance master (b3 candidate to formalize works-council/consent records).

## Modularization hypothesis

Two full modules:
- **INSIDER-RISK-MONITORING-DETECTION**: masters the always-on signal layer: user_risk_scores, monitored_activities, risk_indicators, insider_risk_alerts, detection rules. Consumes DLP/DSPM/UEBA telemetry.
- **INSIDER-RISK-INVESTIGATION-CASE-MGMT**: masters the human workflow: insider_risk_cases (lifecycle), investigation_evidence, exit_risk_assessments (departing-user), HR/Legal handoffs.

This split mirrors how the flagship vendors structure the product (detection plane vs investigation/case plane) and satisfies Rule #14 (at least 3 capabilities means at least 2 full modules).

Distinct, recognized market (Gartner Market Guide + Peer Insights) with at least 3 dedicated flagship vendors. Not a fold-in to DLP/DSPM/SECOPS (those are signal sources / adjacent disciplines). Proceed to Build with cross-domain edges to DLP, DSPM, SECOPS, IGA, HCM.
