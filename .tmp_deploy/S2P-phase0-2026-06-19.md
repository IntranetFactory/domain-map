## Compliance entities

S2P touches SOX (segregation of duties on the approve/pay chain) and mandatory e-invoicing regimes. Basware's surface evidences these as first-class objects; the suite vendors treat them as a sub-feature of invoicing, but the market requires them regardless of vendor presence.

| Entity | Regulation | Proposed module |
|---|---|---|
| sox_audit_trails | SOX (approve-to-pay audit, immutable history) | S2P-INVOICE-CAPTURE (audit), shared with AP-AUTO |
| sod_violations | SOX segregation of duties (requisition vs approve vs pay) | S2P-REQ-TO-PO (approval chain) |
| e_invoices | Peppol / EU structured e-invoicing | S2P-INVOICE-CAPTURE |
| einvoicing_compliance_rules | Country mandate map (100+ countries) | S2P-INVOICE-CAPTURE |

## Modularization hypothesis

**S2P-SOURCING** (master: sourcing_events). Owns the entire upstream e-sourcing seam: rfx_events (RFI/RFP/RFQ), reverse_auctions, supplier_bids, sourcing_awards, savings_records, and award/scenario optimization. Every full-suite vendor (Coupa, Ariba, Ivalua, GEP, JAGGAER, Zip) ships this as a discrete Sourcing module; Basware is the only abstainer. RFP/RFQ canonically live here, not in Procurement.

**S2P-REQ-TO-PO** (masters: purchase_requisitions + purchase_orders, plus intake_requests and purchase_order_change_orders). The transactional buying core. Intake folds here because vendors (Coupa, Ariba, Ivalua, GEP, JAGGAER, Zip) attach intake as a front door to requisitioning, and Zip auto-generates POs directly from the intake object. Two masters in one module is acceptable under Rule #14; intake could be split later if it grows.

**S2P-RECEIVING** (master: goods_receipts). Goods/service receipt is its own document object in all six suite vendors (Ariba adds service_entry_sheets). Vendors place receiving INSIDE the procurement module rather than selling it separately, so this is the weakest standalone-module case (see verdict).

**S2P-INVOICE-CAPTURE** (master: supplier_invoices, the invoice RECORD only). Captures the supplier invoice plus the e-invoicing compliance cluster (SdI, Chorus Pro/Factur-X, CFDI, Peppol). Matching (three_way_matches = AP-AUTO's invoice_matches) and payment_runs are explicitly consumed from the separate AP-AUTO domain, not mastered here. Basware proves this seam is independently large enough to be a market in its own right.

**S2P-CATALOG-MGMT** (masters: procurement_catalogs + catalog_items, plus punch_out_sessions). Hosted catalogs, punch-out, and catalog content. Ariba sells Catalog as a fully separate SKU; the other suites bundle it inside procurement, making this a genuine seam that some vendors externalize and others fold.

**S2P-SPEND-ANALYTICS** (master: spend_categories). Optional. Every full suite ships a separate Spend Analysis module mastering the commodity taxonomy (UNSPSC/custom). Justified as its own module by universal separate-SKU packaging, but non-blocking for the split decision.

## Per-seam verdicts (for the q-file)

**Sourcing seam (STRONG, standalone confirmed).** All six full-suite vendors (Coupa Sourcing+CSO, Ariba Strategic Sourcing Suite, Ivalua Sourcing, GEP Sourcing Management, JAGGAER Sourcing, Zip Sourcing) sell e-sourcing as a discrete module distinct from buying. RFP/RFQ/RFI, reverse auctions, bids, awards, and savings all home here. Verdict: S2P-SOURCING is vendor-supported as its own module. RFP/RFQ canonically live in Sourcing, NOT in Procurement, across the entire vendor set.

**Req-to-PO seam (STRONG, standalone confirmed).** purchase_requisitions + purchase_orders are the transactional spine in every vendor. Intake (intake_requests) folds into this seam rather than being a separate module: Coupa, Ariba, Ivalua, GEP, JAGGAER, and Zip all attach intake as a front door to requisitioning, and Zip auto-generates POs from the intake object. Verdict: keep intake inside S2P-REQ-TO-PO for now (Zip/Coupa show the seam exists but folds into buying).

**Receiving seam (WEAK as standalone, likely FOLD into Req-to-PO).** goods_receipts is a universal object (all six suite vendors plus Basware for matching), but NO vendor sells "receiving" as a separate purchasable module: it sits inside Procurement/P2P (Coupa Procurement, Ariba Buying, Ivalua eProcurement, GEP P2P, JAGGAER eProcurement). Verdict: vendors do NOT support a standalone Receiving module; goods_receipts most defensibly folds into S2P-REQ-TO-PO. Split it out only if the catalog wants receiving-as-capability granularity. This is the main collapse signal in the five-module hypothesis.

**Invoice-Capture seam (STRONG, but draw the capture/match/pay line carefully).** supplier_invoices capture is universal (7/7). The capture-vs-match-vs-pay line differs sharply: Ariba folds invoicing into "Buying AND Invoicing" and stops at a payment_request handed to ERP; Ivalua and Coupa own capture->match->disburse natively; GEP folds all of invoice inside one P2P module; JAGGAER splits Invoicing and Payments; Basware owns capture->three-way-match->e-invoicing-compliance->pay as its entire business. Verdict: S2P masters only the supplier_invoices RECORD plus the e-invoicing compliance cluster; three_way_matches (= AP-AUTO invoice_matches) and payment_runs are consumed from AP-AUTO. The S2P-INVOICE-CAPTURE module is vendor-supported as the invoice system of record; Basware proves the seam is independently substantial.

**Catalog seam (MEDIUM, genuine but split-vs-fold varies by vendor).** Ariba sells Catalog as a fully separate SKU (standalone-module evidence); Coupa, Ivalua, GEP, and JAGGAER fold catalogs+punchout INSIDE their procurement module; Basware offers Marketplace as a Peppol catalog; Zip barely covers it. Verdict: Catalog is its own module in only one vendor (Ariba) and folded into Procurement in four. The catalog can defensibly carve S2P-CATALOG-MGMT (Ariba precedent + distinct objects procurement_catalogs/catalog_items/punch_out_sessions), but a fold into S2P-REQ-TO-PO is the majority-vendor pattern. Recommend keeping it separate to preserve the punch-out/content-management capability cleanly, flagged as a soft seam.

**Overall five-module question.** Vendor evidence cleanly supports Sourcing, Req-to-PO, and Invoice-Capture as three distinct modules. Catalog is a defensible fourth (one vendor sells it separately; objects are distinct). Receiving is the weakest: no vendor sells it standalone, so the honest reading is FOUR strongly-supported modules (Sourcing / Req-to-PO / Invoice-Capture / Catalog) with Receiving folding into Req-to-PO unless capability-granularity is wanted. Spend-Analytics is an optional sixth (universally separate-SKU but analytics-flavored). The domain has well over three capabilities, so Rule #14's >=2-modules floor is comfortably exceeded regardless of how the soft seams resolve.
